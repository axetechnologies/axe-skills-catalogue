JM Cut Paper Marks

Twelve marks, from the archive

This time I looked first. I went through all 132 of Paul Rand's logos, 120 book covers, every poster, and a third of his paintings, and wrote down what is actually there before drawing anything. What I saw was not gloss or icons. It was flat colour, cut paper, one big simple shape, letters cut from blocks, a single visual substitution, hand lettering beside exact geometry, tiny characters built from a few shapes, and repetition with one exception.

What each mark borrows, from a mark I looked at

  • Little Painters and Painter: letters that turn into people (Dunhill's hatted capitals, the Shuredge characters).
  • Mr JM: a face drawn from the letters (the Esquire face, two circles and three lines).
  • Swatch Wall: repetition with an exception (the Earth Day hearts, the American Express squares).
  • Ink Blot, Block Pair, Can Top, Giant jm: a blot, colour blocks (Harcourt Brace), rings (Consolidated Cigar, Columbus) and extreme scale (the Dada and Leger posters).
  • Level, Roller J, One Stroke, Stir Sticks: the four strongest from the last round, redrawn flat on one grid.
